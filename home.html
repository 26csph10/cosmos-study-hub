    Formulas

    Newton's second law: F = m × a
    Universal gravitation: F = G × m1 × m2 / r²
    Kinetic energy: KE = ½ × m × v²
    Mass–energy: E = m × c²
    Wave speed: v = f × λ
    Photon energy: E = h × f
    Escape velocity: v = √(2GM / r)
    Kepler's third law: T² ∝ a³

    Sky guide

    Orion: Winter evenings. Look for the three belt stars in a line.
    The Pleiades: A tight cluster of blue stars, best Oct–Mar.
    Jupiter: Very bright, steady light. Binoculars show its moons.
    The Moon: Best detail near the light/dark edge.

    Exam questions

    Q1. A satellite orbits at radius r. If r doubles, what happens to gravitational force?

    Answer: It becomes ¼ (inverse-square law).

    Q2. Why do stars twinkle but planets mostly don't?

    Answer: Stars are point sources, so the air shifts them more. Planets show a small disc.

    Q3. What does a Hertzsprung–Russell diagram plot?

    Answer: Brightness of stars against their temperature.
